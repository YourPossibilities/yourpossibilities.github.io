About Robin

Robin Rooth-Fogel is a Licensed Clinical Social Worker providing compassionate, evidence-based counseling to individuals and families in the Coopersburg, Pennsylvania area. She works with clients ages 6 through adult across a wide range of concerns.

With a commitment to meeting each client where they are, Robin creates a safe, nonjudgmental space where healing and growth can take root. Her practice is grounded in the belief that every person carries within them the capacity for meaningful change.

Education

Services

Your Possibilities offers counseling services tailored to the unique needs of each client. All sessions are conducted in a confidential, supportive environment.

Individual Counseling

Therapy for ages 6 through adult, addressing anxiety, depression, ADHD, bipolar disorder, OCD, PTSD, trauma, anger management, life transitions, and developmental disabilities.

Family Therapy

Helping families improve dynamics, resolve conflict, and develop healthier patterns of relating to one another.

Adoption & Foster Care

Specialized support for children and caregivers in the adoption and foster care community through Trust-Based Relational Intervention (TBRI) and connection-based approaches.

Community Partnerships

Clinical services through The Salvation Army HALO Project and the Center for Adoption Support and Education (C.A.S.E.).
